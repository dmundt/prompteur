Write a troubleshooting guide for this problem:

{{text}}

- Identify symptoms, affected versions/environments, and known prerequisites.
- Order diagnostic checks from cheapest and safest to more involved.
- Distinguish confirmed causes from hypotheses; explain what each result means.
- Describe reversible recovery, verification, stop conditions, and escalation evidence.
- Avoid secrets, destructive commands, and invented fixes. Update relevant docs only; do not perform recovery.
