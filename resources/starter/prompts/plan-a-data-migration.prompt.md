Plan this data migration:

{{text}}

- Establish source/target formats, data volume, ownership, and availability constraints.
- Identify mappings, validation rules, backups, compatibility, and integrity checks.
- Define ordered steps, dry-run checks, resumability, and duplicate handling.
- Specify rollback prerequisites, stop conditions, and evidence of completion.
- Mark unknowns and ask for blocking details. Plan only; do not run migrations or modify data.
